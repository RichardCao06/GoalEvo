# Security and Research Integrity

Do not commit:

- secrets, credentials, API keys, personal data, or licensed enterprise data;
- hidden goal truth intended for a live blind experiment;
- Gold labels accessible to the Experimental Agent;
- sealed-test generators, seeds, cases, or answer keys in an unprotected path;
- raw Track B evidence without permission and data-rights review.

The current reimbursement fixture is synthetic and explicitly marked Draft. It is not a live sealed benchmark.

Report suspected leakage, unauthorized protocol changes, selective deletion of failures, or compromised sealed material to the human Research Owner and Independent Custodian. Affected confirmatory runs must be quarantined until an incident review determines whether they remain valid.
